OSINT Research Assistant
How it works
Select an indicator on any page, then right-click or press Ctrl+Shift+O
Works with defanged text too — 185.220.101[.]45 and hxxps://evil[.]com are understood.
Or type one straight into the toolbar popup
Results open in a floating panel on the page. The Summary tab answers the question; the other tabs show the detail.
Scan a whole page for indicators
Ctrl+Shift+U, or the button in the popup. Every IP, domain, email and hash on the page gets underlined — click any to look it up.
Supported indicators
IPv4 · IPv6 · Domain · URL · Email · MD5 · SHA-1 · SHA-256
